Key Takeaways

  • Incident volume was highest in 2015 and declined gradually through 2020.
  • California, Texas, and Florida account for the largest shares of recorded incidents.
  • Victims are overwhelmingly male (95%+) and concentrated in the 25–40 age range.
  • The majority of victims were armed; guns represent over half of all cases.
  • Most incidents involved an active threat or attack, with the victim not fleeing.
